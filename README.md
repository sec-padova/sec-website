# Student Entrepreneurs Club

Student Entrepreneurs Club brings people in Padova together around entrepreneurship and startups. It began with students at the University of Padova and welcomes anyone curious about turning ideas into something real.

## Our mission

We make it easier to meet people from different fields, learn from those building ventures, and try ideas together. A student does not need a startup idea or previous experience to take part.

## What we do

- Bring the community together through events and conversations.
- Connect students and other curious people across disciplines.
- Support student led projects and the people working on them.

The club works with the University of Padova and [M31](https://www.m31.com/). Everyone interested in entrepreneurship is welcome to participate.
